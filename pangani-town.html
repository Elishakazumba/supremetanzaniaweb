Pangani Town

A small coastal town where Swahili, Arab and colonial traditions blend with modern hospitality.

A town on the Swahili Coast

Pangani is a small town on East Africa’s coast, 50 km south of Tanga. It has a long history and culture, with Arabic, German, Asian and British colonial influences. It is a place where Swahili, Arab and colonial traditions blend with modern hospitality.

The town sits at the mouth of the Pangani River, which carries water from Mount Kilimanjaro and Mount Meru to the Indian Ocean. Here you will enjoy a coastline of clean beaches where endangered green turtles breed, historic sites, coral reefs, the old port and a great diversity of tropical marine life.

At the point where the massive Pangani River empties into the Indian Ocean, a village has grown. The river passes through the northern side of the town, separating the old buildings and the present-day market from the farms and small houses on the southern side. The river needs a ferry to cross, its dark brown waters heavy with silt as it meanders slowly into the ocean. On either side of the town, coconut palms and sisal plantations undulate towards the horizon.

Once a centre of Swahili trade with the African mainland, Pangani is now a sleepy backwater with few memories of its days of splendour. The old German administrative boma still stands behind a colonnade of tall shade trees, and the former prison, painted a fading ochre red, looks over the river’s lazy waters. Old houses along the main road offer lived-in examples of colonial and traditional Swahili architecture, slowly crumbling against the monsoon winds. Explore what remains of the old town on foot: even a short walk rewards you with a glimpse of quiet life in the old trading towns of the Swahili Coast.

Pangani offers

  • Historical town tour: explore the historic buildings of Pangani, the slave market, the old port and the slave routes.
  • Pangani River cruising.
  • Boat trip to Maziwe Marine Park Island: swimming, sunbathing, snorkelling and watching dolphins.
  • Village tours: a welcome into coastal people’s homes, with a stay with a family and an insight into Swahili culture. Take part in activities with family members.

Green turtle experience

An opportunity to explore the life of endangered green turtles. Visit nesting sites and learn about their breeding behaviour.

Cycling tours

Cycle around the town to the German fort, then on to Mwera, the sisal plantations and Kikokwe, the first place where sisal was introduced to Eastern Africa by Dr. Richard Hindorf, and the adjacent villages. Other cycling tours take you to Mkoma to see the Pemba channel, the Mwanaunguja corals and a bombarded German warship, and on to the beaches of Ushongo.

Fishing with local fishermen

  • Evening tour in and around Pangani town, with an insight into the local entertainment available in the area.
  • Farm tour: visit local farms with coconuts and various fruits, and enjoy fresh fruit and coconut juice among the coconut trees.

Safari to Saadani National Park

You can also travel to Saadani National Park, crossing the Pangani River by ferry (75 km, about 3 hours’ drive), then another 35 km to Saadani village.

Saadani, dubbed “where the bush meets the beach”, is a unique shoreline wilderness that combines marine, savanna, forest and riverine environments with the historic village of Saadani. It lies 70 km north of Bagamoyo.

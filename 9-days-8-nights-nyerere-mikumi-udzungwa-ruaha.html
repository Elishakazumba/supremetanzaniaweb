9 Days / 8 Nights Nyerere, Mikumi, Udzungwa & Ruaha National Park

SafariTrek9 days, 8 nights|Nyerere, Mikumi, Udzungwa and Ruaha

About this trip

Four southern parks in one journey, away from the busier northern circuit.

Itinerary

  1. Day 1: Arrival day

    On arrival at Mwalimu Nyerere International Airport, you will be met by our guide and transferred to your hotel in Dar es Salaam.

    Stay: Four Points Hotel

    Meals: Dinner and breakfast

  2. Day 2: Dar es Salaam to Selous Game Reserve

    In the morning, you will be picked up from your hotel and driven to Selous Game Reserve. Arrive for a hot lunch. In the late afternoon, you will embark on a boat safari on the Rufiji River, where you will encounter hippos and crocodiles in their natural habitat. It is also common to see different species of birds and plants.

    Stay: African Safari Lodge

    Meals: Breakfast, lunch and dinner

  3. Day 3: Selous full day

    After breakfast, you will have a full day of game viewing in Selous Game Reserve with your packed lunch provided. Selous Game Reserve is larger than Switzerland and Denmark. View and explore the wilderness in the biggest game reserve in Africa, with many animals to spot, such as lions, giraffe, wildebeest, zebras, herds of elephants and wild dogs.

    Stay: African Safari Lodge

    Meals: Breakfast, lunch and dinner

  4. Day 4: Walking safari and Mikumi National Park

    In the early morning before breakfast, depart on a 3-hour walking safari in the wilderness, accompanied by an armed ranger guide. You will see various species of birds and plants, which make this a wonderful sanctuary to explore. After breakfast, depart for Mikumi National Park with a packed lunch. The park is nestled between the Uluguru Mountains to the northeast and the Rubeho Mountains, Udzungwa Mountains and Rift Valley escarpment to the southwest. As part of the Selous ecosystem, it benefits from the highest game density of the entire conservation area.

    Stay: Bastian Camp / Mikumi Safari Lodge

    Meals: Breakfast, lunch and dinner

  5. Day 5: Mikumi to Ruaha National Park

    In the early morning after breakfast, we will drive to Ruaha National Park. You will be given the option to go on an evening game drive in this remote area with a dramatic topography of mountains, rivers and plains. Its ragged beauty is only outmatched by the variety and density of wildlife and exotic birdlife.

    Stay: Ruaha Hilltop Lodge

    Meals: Breakfast, lunch and dinner

  6. Day 6: Ruaha National Park (days 6 and 7)

    You will have two full days of game activities in Ruaha National Park. The park covers 12,950 square kilometres, with a large area of dry savanna, and is home to herds of elephants. The forest contains miombo woodland, acacia woodland, riverine forest and terminalia scrub. The Ruaha and Rungwa areas are home to 450 bird species. A hot lunch will be provided.

    Stay: Ruaha Hilltop Lodge

    Meals: Breakfast, lunch and dinner

  7. Day 7: Ruaha to Udzungwa National Park

    In the early morning after breakfast, depart for Udzungwa Mountains National Park with a packed lunch. On arrival, we will begin a mountain hike via the Campsite 3 trail, taking in the sights of forests, vegetation, waterfalls, birds, primates, butterflies, bushbuck, duikers, baboons and monkeys.

    Stay: Udzungwa Forest Lodge

    Meals: Breakfast, lunch and dinner

  8. Day 8: Your departure to Dar es Salaam

    After breakfast you will depart for Dar es Salaam. On arrival, you will be transferred to the airport for your flight back home.

    Meals: Breakfast and boxed lunch

Booking Terms and Conditions

Booking and payment

We confirm your booking once 30% of the total tour price is paid.

The outstanding balance must be paid six (6) weeks before the starting date of your tour.

If the final payment does not reach us by that date, we reserve the right to treat the booking as cancelled.

Last-minute bookings

When a trip is booked after the final payment due date, Supreme Tanzania Safaris requires full payment to confirm your accommodation for your stay in Tanzania.

Cancellation charges

Cancellation charges are assessed as a share of the total tour price, depending on how many days before departure you cancel:

42 to 28 days before departure
50%
27 to 21 days before departure
75%
20 to 0 days before departure
100%

The liability to reimburse the Company for cancellation fees, the non-refundable booking fee, no-show fees, administration fees and the like does not limit or restrict the Company’s general right to claim damages for breach of contract.

Questions about these terms?

We are happy to explain them before you book.

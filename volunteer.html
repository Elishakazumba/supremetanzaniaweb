Volunteer Tourism

Travel that gives something back

At STS, it matters to us that we support and uplift the community in every way we can. That is why we work closely with The Outer Loop Theater Experience on their Humanity Project initiative.

We give travellers the chance to meet a different culture in a more meaningful way, and to gain a new perspective that deepens their understanding of humanity.

Our community projects

Is this the trip for you?

If you want a real sense of purpose and connection in your travels, volunteering may suit you. One of the most valuable parts of this kind of experiential travel is that, when the journey is over, volunteer tourists usually feel they have received far more than they gave to the destination and its people.

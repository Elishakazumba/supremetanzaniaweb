5 Days Marangu Route

TrekKilimanjaro

About this route

The Marangu route starts and ends at Marangu Gate. It climbs through rainforest and heath and moorland, with overnight stops at Mandara, Horombo and Kibo huts, and finishes with a summit attempt from Kibo Hut. This is the five-day itinerary.

Itinerary

  1. Day 1: Marangu Gate (1,860 m) to Mandara Hut (2,700 m)

    After breakfast you are collected from your lodge in Moshi and taken to Marangu Gate. Once the paperwork is done, your trek begins and you soon hike through dense rainforest, with a chance to glimpse some primates on the way to Mandara Hut. After a well-earned rest, your guide can take you to Maundi Crater for a wonderful view of the Kenyan interior.

    Distance: 8.3 km / 5.2 milesTrekking time: About 5 hoursMeals: Breakfast, lunch and dinner

  2. Day 2: Mandara Hut (2,700 m) to Horombo Hut (3,700 m)

    The day begins with an early breakfast. Shortly after leaving Mandara Hut you reach the timberline and enter the heath and moorland zone. About 4 to 6 hours later, Horombo Hut comes into sight. From the hut you have fantastic views over Mawenzi, Kibo and the wide plain of the Maasai steppe.

    Distance: 12.5 km / 7.8 milesTrekking time: About 9 hoursMeals: Breakfast, lunch and dinner

  3. Day 3: Horombo Hut (3,700 m) to Kibo Hut (4,700 m)

    Today’s stage is long and tough. The well-built trail passes the last water point, followed by the saddle, a nearly vegetation-free plateau that joins the main summit Kibo with Mawenzi. Kibo Hut is usually reached in about 5 to 6 hours. You have an early evening meal and an early night, because the night will be short.

    Distance: 9.5 km / 5.9 milesTrekking time: About 7 hoursMeals: Breakfast, lunch and dinner

  4. Day 4: Kibo Hut (4,700 m) to Uhuru Peak (5,895 m), then Horombo Hut (3,700 m)

    Today is the big day, the summit stage. You begin the final ascent around midnight, long and strenuous, passing Hans Meyer Cave (5,220 m). At sunrise you reach Gillman’s Point (5,681 m) on the crater rim, where the sun slowly starts to warm the land. You soon forget the cold of the night, and after a further 1 to 2 hours you reach Uhuru Peak. Back at Kibo Hut a warm meal awaits, followed by a 1 to 2 hour break before you continue down to Horombo Hut.

    Distance: 22 km / 13.7 milesTotal time: 12 to 15 hoursMeals: Breakfast, lunch and dinner

  5. Day 5: Horombo Hut (3,700 m) to Marangu Gate (1,860 m)

    The last stage passes through the heath and moorland zone to Mandara Hut (2,700 m), where a warm lunch is waiting. You then pass through tropical rainforest again and, after about 6 hours of trekking in total, reach the national park gate. After saying goodbye to your mountain guides, a short transfer takes you back to your hotel in Moshi for a warm shower and a celebration of your summit.

    Distance: 20.8 km / 12.9 milesTotal time: About 8 hoursMeals: Breakfast and lunch

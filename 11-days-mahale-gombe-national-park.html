11 Days Mahale & Gombe National Park

Safari11 days|Mahale and Gombe

About this trip

Tanzania's Western Circuit is the perfect place for travellers with a sense of adventure, and the only area where you can see chimpanzees in their natural habitat.

Itinerary

  1. Day 1: Arrival day

    On arrival at Kilimanjaro International Airport you will be met by your guide and transferred to Kibo Palace Hotel in Arusha town.

    Stay: Kibo Palace Hotel

    Meals: Breakfast and dinner

  2. Day 2: Arusha to Kigoma

    In the morning after breakfast you'll be driven to Arusha Airport for your flight to Kigoma. You'll be picked up on arrival in Kigoma and driven to the Lake Tanganyika Beach Hotel for lunch. After lunch you'll visit the Dr Livingstone memorial at Ujiji village where, under the shade of two historical mango trees, the caretaker will regale you with tales about Dr Livingstone and his famous African expedition. As the day comes to an end we'll take you to Kigoma Hilltop Hotel, where you can relax and enjoy the views over Lake Tanganyika as the sun goes down.

    Stay: Kigoma Hilltop Hotel

    Meals: Breakfast, lunch and dinner

  3. Day 3: Kigoma to Mahale Mountains National Park

    In the morning after breakfast you'll be driven to Lake Tanganyika for your boat trip to Mahale Mountains National Park. It takes four to six hours to get from Kigoma town to Mahale by speedboat. After a packed lunch your guide will take you into the forest for late afternoon chimpanzee tracking.

    Stay: Nkungwe Tented Camp

    Meals: Breakfast, boxed lunch and dinner

  4. Day 4: Mahale (days 4 and 5)

    You will spend two full days chimpanzee tracking in Mahale. Chimpanzees normally move in search of food and nest wherever there is an abundant food source, such as their favourite fruit. Snorkelling is an option if you'd like to cool off in the water and see what lies below the surface.

    Stay: Nkungwe Tented Camp

    Meals: Breakfast, lunch and dinner

  5. Day 5: Mahale to Kigoma

    After breakfast, pick up for your boat departure to Kigoma town. On arrival you will be picked up and transferred to the hotel.

    Stay: Kigoma Hilltop Hotel

    Meals: Breakfast, lunch and dinner

  6. Day 6: Kigoma to Gombe

    After breakfast, pick up for your boat departure to Gombe National Park. On arrival you will be picked up and transferred to Gombe Forest Lodge, where you can relax on the beach.

    Stay: Gombe Forest Lodge

    Meals: Breakfast, lunch and dinner

  7. Day 7: Gombe National Park

    Early in the morning after breakfast you'll visit Kakombe waterfall and enjoy another full day of chimp tracking. In the late afternoon you'll head back to the beach for a cool swim in the deepest lake in the world.

    Stay: Gombe Forest Lodge

    Meals: Breakfast, boxed lunch and dinner

  8. Day 8: Gombe National Park

    Chimpanzee tracking, then a visit to Mwamgongo fishing village, where you will enjoy traditional dancing, local music and experience the fishing activities of the villagers.

    Stay: Gombe Forest Lodge

    Meals: Breakfast, lunch and dinner

  9. Day 9: Gombe to Kigoma

    After breakfast, transfer to the lake for your boat departure to Kigoma town. You will be picked up on arrival and transferred to Kigoma Hilltop Hotel.

    Stay: Kigoma Hilltop Hotel

    Meals: Breakfast, lunch and dinner

  10. Day 10: Departure

    After breakfast, pick up and transfer to Kigoma Airport in time for your flight to Dar es Salaam.

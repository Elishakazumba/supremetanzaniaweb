Trek in Tanzania

At Supreme Tanzania Safaris, we handle every detail of your Tanzania trekking adventure with professional care and personal attention. Your journey should be inspiring, well planned and unforgettable from start to finish.

Whether you are chasing a bucket-list summit or enjoying Tanzania on foot, we design trekking itineraries that turn your goals into reality. We specialise in Mount Kilimanjaro, Mount Meru and Ol Doinyo Lengai, and we can combine your trek with wildlife safaris, lodge stays, luxury travel, Zanzibar beach holidays, cultural tours and cycling adventures.

Whether you are an experienced trekker or taking on your first major climb, our team will guide you every step of the way with expert support, local knowledge and warm hospitality. Your next great adventure begins here.

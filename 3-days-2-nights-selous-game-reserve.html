3 Days Selous Game Reserve

Safari3 days, 2 nights|Selous Game Reserve

About this trip

A short safari to one of Africa's largest game reserves, suited to a long weekend.

Itinerary

  1. Day 1: Dar es Salaam to Nyerere National Park (boat safari)

    In the morning you'll be picked up from your hotel and driven to Nyerere National Park (Selous), arriving in time for a hot lunch. In the late afternoon you'll go on a thrilling boat safari on the Rufiji River where you'll see hippos and crocodiles in their natural habitat. Your guide will also be able to point out interesting birds and plant species during this trip.

    Stay: African Safari Lodge / Mloka Lodge

    Meals: Breakfast, lunch and dinner

  2. Day 2: Nyerere full day game drive

    After breakfast, you'll have a full day of game viewing in Nyerere National Park (Selous), taking a packed lunch with you if you wish to maximise your game drive time. Nyerere (Selous) is bigger than Switzerland and Denmark, so it takes a long time to explore properly, but your guide will know the best places to go. These will be special days spent exploring the wilderness in the biggest game reserve in Africa and viewing diverse wildlife which includes lion, giraffe, wildebeest, zebra, elephant, wild dog and so much more. You might even see the rare sable antelope!

    Stay: African Safari Lodge / Mloka Lodge

    Meals: Breakfast, lunch and dinner

  3. Day 3: Nyerere walking safari and departure back to Dar es Salaam

    Leave early morning for a walking safari in the wilderness, accompanied by an armed ranger and a guide who will lead you close to the animals, plants, footprints and animal droppings for fine pictures. Then drive back to your lodge at 10:00 for breakfast. Afterwards, check out from the lodge and drive back to your hotel in Dar es Salaam.

    Meals: Breakfast and boxed lunch

Community Social Responsibility

At Supreme Tanzania Safaris, we feel that it’s very important to give back to our community. That’s why we partner with organizations like The Outer Loop Theater Experience & The Humanity Project, in the U.S, to help bring the arts, education, clean water and healthcare to villages in need throughout Tanzania. A portion of all Wildlife Safari proceeds go to making sure that those who need an education and access to proper medical care can afford them both.

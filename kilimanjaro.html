Mount Kilimanjaro National Park

At 5,895 metres, Kilimanjaro is Africa’s highest mountain and the tallest free-standing mountain in the world.

The roof of Africa

Kilimanjaro has three main volcanic peaks: Kibo, Mawenzi and Shira. Its name is a mystery wreathed in clouds. It might mean Mountain of Light, Mountain of Greatness or Mountain of Caravans. The local Wachagga people have no name for the whole massif, only Kipoo (now Kibo) for the snowy peak that watches over the continent.

The mountain rises above the rolling hills of northern Tanzania near the town of Moshi, its slopes and glaciers shimmering above the clouds. It is a protected area, carefully regulated so climbers can enjoy it without leaving a trace. Climbing it is the highlight of many visitors’ time in Tanzania. If you pace yourself well, everyone from seasoned trekkers to first-time enthusiasts can reach the snowy summit.

Most climbers reach the crater rim with little more than a walking stick, proper clothing and determination. Those who reach Gillman’s Point on the crater rim, or Uhuru Point, the true summit, earn their climbing certificates. And their memories.

A climate tour in one climb

The ascent is a journey from the tropics to the Arctic. On the lower slopes, farmland grows coffee, bananas, cassava and maize. Inside the park boundary, at about 2,700 metres, lush montane forest is home to elusive elephant, leopard, buffalo, the endangered Abbott’s duiker and other small antelope and primates. Higher up is moorland, with giant heather and otherworldly giant lobelias.

Above 4,000 metres is a surreal alpine desert where only a few hardy mosses and lichens survive. Then the last vegetation gives way to ice and snow, and the view from the roof of the continent stretches across the Rift Valley, the Maasai Steppe and Kenya’s Amboseli National Park.

What to do

  • Six usual trekking routes to the summit, plus more demanding mountaineering routes.
  • Day or overnight hikes on the Shira plateau, and nature trails on the lower reaches.
  • Trout fishing.
  • A visit to the beautiful Chala crater lake on the south-eastern slopes.

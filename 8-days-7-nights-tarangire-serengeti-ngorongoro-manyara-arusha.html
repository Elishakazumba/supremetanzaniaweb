8 Days / 7 Nights Tarangire, Serengeti, Ngorongoro, Lake Manyara & Arusha National Park

Safari8 days, 7 nights|Northern circuit

About this trip

A long northern circuit that takes in five parks, from Tarangire to Arusha National Park.

Itinerary

  1. Day 1: Arrive in Arusha

    Arrive at Kilimanjaro International Airport, where you will be picked up and transferred to Gold Crest Hotel for the night.

    Stay: Gold Crest Hotel

    Meals: Breakfast, packed lunch and dinner

  2. Day 2: Arusha to Tarangire National Park

    After breakfast, depart for Tarangire National Park, famous for its tree-climbing pythons, massive baobabs and large herds of elephant. Tarangire is small but still home to a significant wildlife population, with a density of wildlife matched only by Ngorongoro. Picnic lunch.

    Stay: African Safari Manyara Lodge

    Meals: Breakfast, packed lunch and dinner

  3. Day 3: Serengeti National Park

    Early in the morning, after breakfast, depart for the Serengeti with a packed lunch. The Serengeti is the oldest and most popular national park, known for its annual migration, in which wildebeest, zebra and Thomson's gazelle join the trek for fresh grazing. Animals found in the Serengeti include buffalo, giraffe, eland, topi, kongoni, impala, Grant's gazelle, lion, cheetah, leopard and African jackal.

    Stay: Seronera Wildlife Lodge

    Meals: Breakfast, packed lunch and dinner

  4. Day 4: Serengeti full day

    After breakfast, continue with a full day of migration viewing in Serengeti National Park with a packed lunch. Serengeti National Park is widely regarded as the best wildlife reserve in Africa due to its density of predators and prey, with the Great Migration to watch.

    Stay: Seronera Wildlife Lodge

    Meals: Breakfast, packed lunch and dinner

  5. Day 5: Ngorongoro Crater

    Just after breakfast, drive and descend into the Ngorongoro Crater for your early game drive. The main feature of the Ngorongoro Conservation Area is the Crater, a large, unbroken, unflooded volcanic caldera. It formed when a giant volcano exploded and collapsed on itself some two to three million years ago. It is 610 m (2,000 ft) deep and its floor covers 260 km2 (100 sq mi). Estimates of the height of the original volcano range from fifteen to nineteen thousand feet (4,500 to 5,800 metres). Animal populations in the crater include most of the species found in East Africa: lion, elephant, zebra, rhino, leopard, buffalo, gazelle, wildebeest, hippo and many species of birds.

    Stay: African Safari Karatu Lodge

    Meals: Breakfast, packed lunch and dinner

  6. Day 6: Lake Manyara National Park

    After breakfast, depart for Lake Manyara National Park. The park covers 127 square miles (329 km2), and the lake's alkaline waters cover approximately 89 square miles (231 km2). While most known for baboons, the lake and its environment is also home to herbivores such as hippos, impala, elephants, wildebeest, buffalo, warthogs and giraffes. Giant fig trees and mahogany in the groundwater forest immediately around the park gates draw nourishment from underground springs replenished continuously from the crater highlands directly above the Manyara basin. The park is also very famous for its tree-climbing lions.

    Stay: African Safari Manyara Lodge

    Meals: Breakfast, packed lunch and dinner

  7. Day 7: Arusha National Park

    After breakfast, depart for the small but beautiful Arusha National Park, the closest national park to both the famous Arusha "safari town" (30 km) and Kilimanjaro International Airport. You will do an exciting morning game drive and afternoon game drive, visiting both the Momela Lakes and the Ngurdoto Crater. Wildlife you might see includes African buffalo, elephants, hippos, giraffe, warthogs, antelopes, zebras, blue monkeys and sometimes a rare leopard or hyena.

    Stay: Momella Wildlife Lodge

    Meals: Breakfast, packed lunch and dinner

  8. Day 8: Lake Manyara to Kilimanjaro International Airport

    After breakfast, check out from the lodge and transfer to Kilimanjaro International Airport for your flight back home. End of tour.

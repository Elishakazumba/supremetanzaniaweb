8 Day Spectacular Migration Serengeti

Safari8 days|Serengeti

About this trip

You'll stay in remote and beautiful safari accommodation, including Lobo Wildlife Lodge, where an array of wildlife comes up to the lodge day and night because of its floodlit water hole.

Itinerary

  1. Day 1: Arrival day

    Arrive at Kilimanjaro International Airport, where you will be picked up and transferred to your hotel in Arusha town for the night.

    Stay: African Safari Arusha Lodge

    Meals: Dinner

  2. Day 2: Lake Manyara National Park

    After breakfast, depart for Lake Manyara National Park. The park covers 127 square miles (329 km2), and the lake's alkaline waters cover approximately 89 square miles (231 km2). While most known for baboons, the lake and its environment is also home to herbivores such as hippos, impala, elephants, wildebeest, buffalo, warthogs and giraffes. Giant fig trees and mahogany in the groundwater forest immediately around the park gates draw nourishment from underground springs replenished continuously from the crater highlands directly above the Manyara basin. The park is also very famous for its tree-climbing lions.

    Stay: African Safari Manyara Lodge

    Meals: Breakfast, lunch and dinner

  3. Day 3: Serengeti National Park

    Early in the morning, after breakfast, depart for the Serengeti via the Ngorongoro Conservation Area with a packed lunch. The Serengeti is the oldest and most popular national park, known for its annual migration, in which wildebeest, zebra and Thomson's gazelle join the trek for fresh grazing. Animals found in the Serengeti include buffalo, giraffe, eland, topi, kongoni, impala, Grant's gazelle, lion, cheetah, leopard and African jackal.

    Stay: African Safari Serengeti Lodge

    Meals: Breakfast, lunch and dinner

  4. Day 4: Serengeti full days (days 4 and 5)

    You will spend 2 full days viewing the migration in Serengeti National Park with a packed lunch. Serengeti National Park is widely regarded as the best wildlife reserve in Africa due to its density of predators and prey, with the Great Migration to watch.

    Stay: Ang'ata Camp

    Meals: Breakfast, lunch and dinner

  5. Day 5: Serengeti to Ngorongoro (Maasai boma)

    Just after breakfast, enjoy a game drive in the Serengeti. After lunch, depart for Ngorongoro, passing a Maasai boma, and stay overnight at Rhino Lodge.

    Stay: Ngorongoro Rhino Lodge

    Meals: Breakfast, lunch and dinner

  6. Day 6: Ngorongoro Crater

    After breakfast, drive and descend into the Ngorongoro Crater for your game drive. The main feature of Ngorongoro is the Crater, a large, unbroken, unflooded volcanic caldera. It formed when a giant volcano exploded and collapsed on itself some two to three million years ago. It is 610 m (2,000 ft) deep and its floor covers 260 km2 (100 sq mi). Estimates of the height of the original volcano range from fifteen to nineteen thousand feet (4,500 to 5,800 metres). Animal populations in the crater include most of the species found in East Africa: lion, elephant, zebra, rhino, leopard, buffalo, gazelle, wildebeest, hippo and many species of birds.

    Stay: African Safari Karatu

    Meals: Breakfast, lunch and dinner

  7. Day 7: Ngorongoro to Kilimanjaro Airport

    After breakfast, check out from the lodge and drive to Arusha, stopping for souvenirs at the Cultural Heritage Centre. You will then have lunch at Shanga Shangaa restaurant. After lunch you will be transferred to the airport for your flight back home.

    Meals: Breakfast and lunch

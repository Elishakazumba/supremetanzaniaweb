7 Days Rongai Route

TrekKilimanjaro

About this route

The Rongai route is the only route that approaches Kilimanjaro from the north, close to the Kenyan border. It is gaining popularity but still has low traffic, which makes it a good alternative to the crowded Marangu route. It suits climbers who want a more remote hike and those climbing in the rainy season, because the north side gets less rain. The scenery is not as varied as on the western routes, but Rongai makes up for it by passing through true wilderness for days before it joins the Marangu route at Kibo Hut and descends by that route. It is a moderately difficult route and is highly recommended, especially for climbers with less backpacking experience. The minimum is six days, and seven days are recommended.

Itinerary

  1. Day 1: Nalemoru Gate (1,990 m) to Simba Camp (2,625 m)

    You drive to the village of Nale Moru, the starting point of the Rongai route. The first stage of the trek is through farmland and pine forest. You may see colobus monkeys and, with luck, elephants or buffalo. The first camp, Simba Camp, sits at the edge of the moorland zone.

    Distance: 8 km / 5 milesTrekking time: 4 to 5 hoursMeals: Breakfast, lunch and dinner

  2. Day 2: Simba Camp (2,625 m) to Second Cave Camp (3,480 m)

    A steady morning ascent to Second Cave Camp in the moorland zone, with superb views of Kibo and the eastern ice fields on the crater rim.

    Distance: 5 km / 3.1 milesTrekking time: 3 to 4 hoursMeals: Breakfast, lunch and dinner

  3. Day 3: Second Cave Camp (3,480 m) to Kikelewa Camp (3,630 m)

    You leave the pine forest behind and keep climbing steadily through moorland, with superb views of the eastern ice fields on the crater rim of Kibo, the highest of the mountain’s three volcanoes. You then leave the main trail and strike out across the moorland on a smaller path towards the jagged peaks of Mawenzi, the second of Kilimanjaro’s volcanoes. Camp is in a sheltered valley near Kikelewa Caves.

    Distance: 5 km / 3.1 milesTrekking time: 3 to 4 hoursMeals: Breakfast, lunch and dinner

  4. Day 4: Kikelewa Camp (3,630 m) to Mawenzi Tarn Hut (4,310 m)

    A short but steep climb up grassy slopes, rewarded with a superb view of the Kenyan plains to the north. You leave the vegetation behind shortly before reaching Mawenzi Tarn Hut, directly below the towering cliffs of Mawenzi. In the afternoon you can rest or explore the area to help your acclimatisation.

    Distance: 8 km / 5 milesTrekking time: 4 to 5 hoursMeals: Breakfast, lunch and dinner

  5. Day 5: Mawenzi Tarn Hut (4,310 m) to Kibo Hut (4,700 m)

    You cross the lunar desert of the saddle between Mawenzi and Kibo. After a couple of hours you reach a spectacular spot, with the summit of Kilimanjaro behind you and the trail to high camp stretched out ahead. The rest of the day is spent resting, with a very early night before the final ascent.

    Distance: 8 km / 5 milesTrekking time: 5 to 6 hoursMeals: Breakfast, lunch and dinner

  6. Day 6: Kibo Hut (4,700 m) to Uhuru Peak (5,895 m), then Horombo Hut (3,700 m)

    Today is the big day, the summit stage. You begin the final ascent around midnight, long and strenuous, passing Hans Meyer Cave (5,220 m). At sunrise you reach Gillman’s Point (5,681 m) on the crater rim, where the sun slowly starts to warm the land. You soon forget the cold of the night, and after a further 1 to 2 hours you reach Uhuru Peak. Back at Kibo Hut a warm meal awaits, followed by a 1 to 2 hour break before you continue down to Horombo Hut.

    Distance: 22 km / 13.7 milesTotal time: 12 to 15 hoursMeals: Breakfast, lunch and dinner

  7. Day 7: Horombo Hut (3,700 m) to Marangu Gate (1,860 m)

    The last stage passes through the heath and moorland zone to Mandara Hut (2,700 m), where a warm lunch is waiting. You then pass through tropical rainforest again and, after about 6 hours of trekking in total, reach Kilimanjaro National Park gate. After saying goodbye to your mountain guides, a short transfer takes you back to your hotel in Moshi for a warm shower and a celebration of your summit.

    Distance: 20.8 km / 12.9 milesTotal time: About 8 hoursMeals: Breakfast and lunch

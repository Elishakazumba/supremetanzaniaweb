6 Days Rongai Route

TrekKilimanjaro

About this route

The Rongai route is the only route that approaches Kilimanjaro from the north, close to the Kenyan border. It is gaining popularity but still has low traffic, which makes it a good alternative to the crowded Marangu route. It suits climbers who want a more remote hike and those climbing in the rainy season, because the north side gets less rain. The scenery is not as varied as on the western routes, but Rongai makes up for it by passing through true wilderness for days before it joins the Marangu route at Kibo Hut and descends by that route. It is a moderately difficult route and is highly recommended, especially for climbers with less backpacking experience. The minimum is six days, and seven days are recommended.

Itinerary

  1. Day 1: Nalemoru Gate (1,990 m) to Simba Camp (2,625 m)

    You drive to the village of Nale Moru, the starting point of the Rongai route. The first stage of the trek is through farmland and pine forest. You may see colobus monkeys and, with luck, elephants or buffalo. The first camp, Simba Camp, sits at the edge of the moorland zone.

    Distance: 8 km / 5 milesTrekking time: 4 to 5 hoursMeals: Breakfast, lunch and dinner

  2. Day 2: Simba Camp (2,625 m) to Kikelewa Camp (3,679 m)

    You hike from Simba Camp up towards Kibo to First Caves (3,482 m) for lunch, a distance of 5.8 km. After lunch you continue to Kikelewa Camp. The views begin to open up and you start to feel you are on a really big mountain. There is a stream below the cave, although you may have to walk a little way downhill to find a pool or two.

    Distance: About 11.8 kmTrekking time: 6 to 7 hoursMeals: Breakfast, lunch and dinner

  3. Day 3: Kikelewa Camp (3,679 m) to Mawenzi Tarn Camp (4,303 m)

    A short but steep climb up grassy slopes with superb views of this wilderness area. The vegetation ends shortly before you reach Mawenzi Tarn Camp, spectacularly set beneath the towering spires of Mawenzi. Spend the afternoon acclimatising and exploring the area.

    Distance: 8 km / 5 milesTrekking time: 4 to 5 hoursMeals: Breakfast, lunch and dinner

  4. Day 4: Mawenzi Tarn Camp (4,303 m) to Kibo Hut (4,730 m)

    After breakfast you head west, continuing up the east side of Kibo and crossing the saddle between Mawenzi and Kibo. It takes 4 to 5 hours to reach Kibo Hut. You spend the rest of the day resting before the final ascent, which begins around midnight.

    Distance: 8 km / 5 milesTrekking time: 5 to 6 hoursMeals: Breakfast, lunch and dinner

  5. Day 5: Kibo Hut (4,730 m) to Uhuru Peak (5,895 m), then Horombo Hut (3,705 m)

    You rise around 11:20 pm and, after tea and biscuits, set off into the night, where the going really gets tough. The first section is a rocky path to Hans Meyer Cave (5,150 m), a good resting spot. The path then zigzags up to Gillman’s Point (5,681 m) on the crater rim. This steep, stony section is probably the most demanding of the whole route and takes great physical and mental effort, so move slowly. From Gillman’s Point you will normally find snow all the way to Uhuru Peak, the highest point in Africa. Weather on the summit decides how long you can stay for photographs before the 3-hour descent to Kibo Hut. After a short rest there, you collect the gear you left behind and continue down to Horombo Hut (about 3 hours) for the night. The descent feels surprisingly fast compared to the climb. In total you walk for around 14 hours, so be prepared for a very tough day.

    Time to Uhuru Peak: 7 to 8 hoursTime to descend to Horombo: 6 to 8 hoursTotal walking: 12 to 15 hoursMeals: Breakfast, lunch and dinner

  6. Day 6: Horombo Hut (3,705 m) to Marangu Gate (1,860 m)

    After breakfast you continue the descent, passing Mandara Hut, down to Marangu Gate. We strongly recommend not tipping your porters until you and all your gear have reached the gate safely. At the gate you sign the register and successful climbers receive their certificates: green for Gillman’s Point, gold for Uhuru Peak. You then drive back to Moshi for a long-awaited hot shower, dinner and celebrations.

    Distance: About 29 kmHiking time: 6 hoursMeals: Breakfast and lunch

About Supreme Tanzania Safaris

Your trusted partner for unforgettable journeys across Tanzania and East Africa.

Who We Are

A Tanzanian tour operator built around your trip

Supreme Tanzania Safaris is a tour operator based in Dar es Salaam. We design and run safaris, Kilimanjaro treks and Zanzibar escapes across Tanzania, tailored to your interests and your budget.

Whether you have a long weekend or two weeks, we take time to understand what you want to see and do, then shape an itinerary around it. Wildlife drives, guided walks, birding, cultural visits and beach days can all be part of the plan.

We can also help with domestic flights, car hire and onward trips to neighbouring countries, so everything is arranged in one place.

What We Do

Safaris, summits and shores

Wildlife Safaris

Serengeti, Ngorongoro Crater, Tarangire, Selous, Mikumi and Ruaha, planned around the season and the animals you most want to see.

Kilimanjaro & Mountain Treks

Guided climbs on every major Kilimanjaro route, plus Mount Meru and Ol Doinyo Lengai for trekkers of all levels.

Zanzibar & Coast

Beach extensions, Stone Town, Pangani and Mafia Island, easy to add after a safari or to enjoy on their own.

Tailor-Made Trips

Tell us your dates, interests and budget and we build a programme around them, from a long weekend to a two-week holiday.

Our Team

The people behind your trip

Samwel Ludamila

Founder & CEO

Samwel is one of the top tour organizers in Tanzania. He guides tours himself, runs the company’s daily operations and oversees all of the Humanity Project’s work in Tanzania.

Kapande Hassan

Professional Driver Guide

Kapande has extensive knowledge and experience of the wildlife, nature and local cultures across Tanzania, and speaks English and the local languages fluently.

Michael Herman

Tour Organizer, U.S. Operations

Michael founded The Outer Loop Theater Experience and co-directs The Humanity Project. For Supreme he handles U.S. tour organizing, travel agency liaison, marketing, and logistics for student and group trips.

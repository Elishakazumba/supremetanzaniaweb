Mount Meru Trekking

About Mount Meru

At 4,566 metres, Mount Meru is the fifth highest mountain on the African continent and the second highest in Tanzania. About 250,000 years ago, a massive volcanic explosion swept away the entire eastern flank of the mountain and left it with the distinctive shape it has today. Its last eruption was in 1910.

Changing landscapes

The ascent passes through several vegetation zones. Forest on the lower slopes gives way to dense mountain rainforest, which turns into bush. Towards the top, moorland takes over and is then replaced by alpine desert.

The wildlife changes with the plants. On the way up you can meet large mammals such as elephants and buffalo, and sometimes leopards. For this reason you must be accompanied by an armed park ranger throughout the trek.

6 Days Machame Route

TrekKilimanjaro

About this route

The Machame route starts at Machame Gate, about an hour’s drive from Moshi. It climbs through dense rainforest, crosses the Shira plateau and the Great Barranco Wall, and makes its summit attempt from Barafu Camp. It follows the mountaineering code of ‘walk high, sleep low’ to help your body adjust to the altitude. This is the six-day itinerary.

Itinerary

  1. Day 1: Machame Gate (1,790 m) to Machame Camp (3,010 m)

    The gate is an hour’s drive from Moshi. There you complete park formalities, meet your crew of porters and start to climb. The first section climbs steadily through magnificent, dense rainforest. The path is less well trodden, so it can be overgrown in places and is often wet and muddy underfoot.

    Distance: 10.8 km / 6.7 milesTrekking time: About 6 hoursMeals: Breakfast, lunch and dinner

  2. Day 2: Machame Camp (3,010 m) to Shira Camp (3,845 m)

    The route continues through the forest until the steep ascent onto the Shira plateau, with rewarding views of the mountain. Looking back, you can see Mount Meru rising high above Arusha in the distance.

    Distance: 5.4 km / 3.4 milesTrekking time: About 5 hoursMeals: Breakfast, lunch and dinner

  3. Day 3: Shira Camp (3,845 m) to Barranco Camp (3,960 m)

    Walking on high moorland changes the whole character of the trek. You traverse the south-west side of Kilimanjaro, passing underneath Lava Tower and the final section of the Western Breach, before reaching camp. The day is spent at altitude, up to 4,600 m, but following the code of walk high, sleep low helps your body acclimatise.

    Distance: 10.8 km / 6.7 milesTrekking time: About 8 hoursMeals: Breakfast, lunch and dinner

  4. Day 4: Barranco Camp (3,960 m) to Barafu Camp (4,640 m)

    The day starts with a descent into the Great Barranco, a huge ravine, then a steep climb up the Great Barranco Wall, which separates you from the south-eastern slopes of Kibo. It is a long, tiring climb over rock, though not technical. Passing beneath the Heim and Kersten glaciers, you head towards the Karanga valley, your last stop for fresh water before the summit. The terrain then turns to scree as you walk through arid, desolate land to Barafu Camp.

    Distance: 8.5 km / 5.3 milesTrekking time: About 8 hoursMeals: Breakfast, lunch and dinner

  5. Day 5: Barafu Camp (4,640 m) to Uhuru Peak (5,895 m), then Millennium Camp (3,790 m)

    You start around midnight and walk steeply up to the summit glaciers. You climb scree for 4 to 5 hours, gaining incredible height over a short distance, with spectacular views. You should be on the crater rim at Stella Point (5,739 m) as the first rays of the sun arrive. Spectacular ice cliffs surround you in the crater, with breathtaking views to jagged Mawenzi and beyond. Another hour’s walk brings you to the summit, Uhuru Peak. You return to Stella Point, descend the scree slope back to Barafu Camp for breakfast, and finally go down to Millennium Camp for a long, well-earned rest.

    Distance: 13.4 km / 8.3 milesTotal time: 12 to 15 hoursMeals: Breakfast, lunch and dinner

  6. Day 6: Millennium Camp (3,790 m) to Mweka Gate (1,630 m)

    A gentle trek down through the rainforest to Mweka Gate, where you complete park formalities and receive your certificates, ready to hang up with pride. Our vehicles meet you and take you back to your hotel in Moshi for a welcome hot shower.

    Distance: 12.1 km / 7.5 milesTrekking time: About 6 hoursMeals: Breakfast and lunch

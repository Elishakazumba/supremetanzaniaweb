6 Days Kili Marangu Route

TrekKilimanjaro

About this route

The Marangu route, also known as the Coca-Cola route, is the most popular route up Kilimanjaro, which is how it got its name. It is a well-travelled path through the rainforest at Marangu village, into the heath and moorland, and then into the alpine desert of the saddle between Mawenzi and Kibo, on to the crater rim at Gillman’s Point. Only about half of all trekkers reach Gillman’s Point. This six-day itinerary adds an acclimatisation day at Horombo Hut.

Itinerary

  1. Day 1: Marangu Gate (1,800 m) to Mandara Hut (2,700 m)

    You leave Arusha at 8 am and drive for about 2 hours to Marangu village. At the gate, after the administrative formalities and meeting your porters and guides, you begin the 4 to 5 hour walk to Mandara Hut. The walk is at a leisurely pace through dense, humid forest. If you feel up to it at the end of the day, you can walk to the nearby Maundi Crater for a long view over the plains and Lake Jipe, and in the distance the Pare and Usambara mountains. The night is spent in one of the huts.

    Walking time: 4 to 5 hours

  2. Day 2: Mandara Hut to Horombo Hut (3,720 m)

    After about 45 minutes in the forest you arrive at an area of high grasses in the heather belt, which becomes increasingly desert-like as you climb into the moorland. About 5 to 6 hours of walking brings you to Horombo Hut and a stunning view of Mawenzi Peak. The night is spent in one of the huts.

    Walking time: 5 to 6 hours

  3. Day 3: Acclimatisation day at Horombo Hut

    This extra day at Horombo Hut helps your body acclimatise and lets you enjoy the scenery. There is a trek to the base of Mawenzi at nearly 4,600 m, passing Zebra Rock and the striking landscape of the moorland zone.

  4. Day 4: Horombo Hut to Kibo Hut (4,703 m)

    Trekking takes about 6 to 7 hours, across the exposed, barren alpine desert of the saddle between Kibo and Mawenzi. The last fresh water point is at about 4,300 m, where you need to fill your water bottles. The porters carry the rest of the water for food and hot drinks. The saddle crossing is impressive and the landscape spectacular. The night is spent at Kibo Hut.

    Walking time: 6 to 7 hours

  5. Day 5: Kibo Hut to Uhuru Peak (5,895 m), then Horombo Hut (3,720 m)

    You are woken early with a steaming cup of coffee or tea and leave between 12:30 and 1 am. Temperatures range from just below freezing at midnight to between -12°C and -23°C just before dawn at Gillman’s Point (5,685 m) on the crater rim. The winding scree path of endless switchbacks carries you through the night, and at dawn the spires of Mawenzi are silhouetted against the red glow of the sun beneath the clouds. After about 6 hours of walking you reach the edge of the crater, near the Ratzel Glacier. From Gillman’s Point, summon your strength for another 1.5 hours over a gentle slope around the crater rim to Uhuru Peak. After a short time at the summit, you descend the steep scree trail to Kibo Hut for some rest, then go on down to Horombo Hut for the night.

    Walking time to Gillman’s Point: About 6 hoursGillman’s Point to Uhuru: About 1.5 hours

  6. Day 6: Horombo Hut to Marangu Gate (1,800 m), then Arusha

    You descend straight to Marangu Gate, about 6 hours of walking. At the gate you have lunch and are then transferred to Arusha for a much-welcome shower.

    Walking time: About 6 hours
